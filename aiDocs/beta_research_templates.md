# HabitDx Beta Research Templates

**Last Updated:** March 23, 2026

---

## 1. Recruitment Message

Use for LinkedIn, email, or private outreach:

> I’m looking for 20-30 beta testers for HabitDx, a habit app built for people who have tried habit trackers before and bounced off them. The beta runs for 4 weeks and includes a few short feedback check-ins. I’m especially looking for knowledge workers who have tried 2+ habit apps and want better insight into why habits fail. If that sounds like you, I’d love to send the screener.

---

## 2. Screener Form

### Must-have questions
- How many habit tracking apps have you tried before?
- Which habit apps have you used?
- Are you currently using any habit tracking app?
- What usually causes you to stop using them?
- Are you willing to use a new app for 4 weeks?
- Are you willing to do up to 3 short feedback interviews?
- What is your job or primary day-to-day work?

### Selection rules
- Prioritize testers who used `2+` habit apps
- Prioritize testers not currently satisfied with a competitor
- Prioritize testers willing to join a community channel and complete all 3 touchpoints

---

## 3. Interview Guide

### Touchpoint 1: 24 Hours After Onboarding
- What was your first reaction to the onboarding flow?
- Which screen felt slow, unclear, or too personal?
- Did the Failure Profile feel specific to you?
- Which part of the profile felt strongest? weakest?
- Do the suggested habits fit your real life this week?
- What almost stopped you from continuing?

### Touchpoint 2: Week 1
- How many days did you open HabitDx this week?
- Which habit felt easiest to keep? hardest?
- Did reminders help or annoy you?
- When you missed, what got in the way?
- Are you planning to keep using the app next week? why?

### Touchpoint 3: Week 4
- Did the weekly insight feel earned from your data?
- Did you accept the recommendation? why or why not?
- What would have made the adjustment more believable?
- Has the app changed how you think about failing at habits?
- Would you recommend HabitDx to a friend? why or why not?

---

## 4. Exit Survey

Send when a tester has been inactive for 5-7 days or explicitly drops out.

### Required questions
- What best describes why you stopped using HabitDx?
- At what point did the product stop feeling useful?
- Did the app feel too generic, too demanding, or not relevant enough?
- Did notifications help, annoy, or not matter?
- What one change would most increase your likelihood of returning?
- On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend HabitDx?

### Suggested answer tags
- onboarding too long
- habits did not fit
- insights not useful
- forgot to come back
- notifications poor
- bugs or reliability
- privacy or trust concern

---

## 5. Community Channel Setup

### Recommended channels
- `#welcome`
- `#announcements`
- `#bug-reports`
- `#weekly-wins`
- `#feedback`

### Community rules
- Keep it supportive and specific
- Report bugs with screenshot, device, and app version
- Share friction honestly
- Avoid giving advice to other testers as if it is official guidance

### Moderator checklist
- Acknowledge every new report within 24 hours
- Tag each item by type and severity
- Close the loop when feedback leads to a product change
